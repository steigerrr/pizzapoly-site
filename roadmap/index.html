Building the next shift.

Here's the direction we're exploring for Pizzapoly, from the playable core to a bigger restaurant experience. Priorities and scope can change based on development and community feedback.

Core experience

Run the restaurant

The foundation of Pizzapoly's hands-on co-op restaurant gameplay.

  • 1–4 player online co-op
  • Pizza preparation and baking
  • Serving and packaging
  • Inventory and restocking
  • Cleaning and deliveries
Early Access focus

Improve every shift

Areas we intend to refine with player feedback.

  • Co-op balance and coordination
  • Restaurant workflow improvements
  • Difficulty and pacing adjustments
  • Progression tuning
  • Quality-of-life improvements
Longer-term vision

Grow the business

Ideas and features planned for the broader game experience.

  • New neighborhoods and areas
  • More recipes and side dishes
  • Deeper restaurant management
  • Multiple restaurant branches
  • Possible property acquisitions

This is a directional roadmap, not a release schedule or guarantee that every proposed feature will ship.
