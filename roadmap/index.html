Roadmap

Phase 01

Expand the kitchen

Make room in the kitchen for a bigger service.

  • Restaurant equipment upgrades, bigger fridges, an extra oven and oven upgrades.
  • Rearrange kitchen equipment and work with NPC staff.
  • Carry ingredients more easily.
  • A reworked tutorial flow, further tutorial improvements and a clearer interface.

Phase 02

Bring new flavors to the menu

New items at the counter, new jobs at the oven.

  • New pizza recipes, ingredients and products.
  • New drinks and desserts.
  • New kitchen interactions and small additions to cooking.

Phase 03

Get the crew together

Call your friends, pick your character and clock in.

  • Public lobbies and in-game voice chat.
  • Choose from six preset characters or customize your character.
  • New social interactions and animations, plus multiplayer pause.
  • Sessions for 6–8 players.

Phase 04

Make room for regulars

A new vibe for the restaurant: a relaxed service or a spin around town.

  • Regular customers, fun new customer interactions and new ways to interact with restaurant décor.
  • New sound effects and character reactions, plus a Twitch prototype that tries out viewer names for customers.
  • Chill Mode: More patient customers and a relaxed pace.
  • More vehicles and customization options.
  • Further testing on Linux, Proton and Steam Deck.
  • A secret mode: Its name and rules stay in the kitchen for now.

Plans may change. This roadmap is not a release schedule.
